# Owner Split — Product Brief

## Who & what
Anton and Taras share one project and one plugin, from different machines (Anton on a Mac,
Taras on Linux). Each wants the tools to suggest and start only their own work plus work
nobody owns, never the other person's.

## Why
On 2026-10-17 Anton's crew started workers on Taras's specs (cross-studio, the staff iOS
release). The overseer had to stop them by hand, then keep "skip Taras's specs" notes that
later ticks still ignored. Those specs already say they are Taras's; nothing listens.

## What we'll build
- Whoever runs the crew gets workers only on their own specs and unowned ones.
- The overseer's ready list and every "Next sessions" suggestion follow the same rule.
- Starting someone else's spec through the crew by hand is refused, naming the owner.
- If the tools can't tell who is running them, they say so instead of picking everything.
- "What is Taras working on?" (or Anton) gets a real answer: the specs that person owns,
  and what they are actually doing right now, wherever their machine is.
- It works the same on both machines with no per-person setup.

## The real change
"Who am I" becomes an input to every suggestion and every crew pick: work is offered to
its owner, and unowned work to anyone. "Who is on what" becomes a question anyone can ask.

## Where it touches (product level)
- The crew: its automatic refill and a person's hand launch through it
- The overseer's start message and ready list
- "Next sessions" at the end of prep, create, review and handoff
- Asking what a person is working on (today this only covers the focus list)

## Out of scope
- Hiding anything from the board: it still shows everyone's work, owner marked
- Blocking a person who deliberately opens someone else's spec in their own session
- Assigning owners to existing specs, or cleaning the overseer's run file (done by hand)
- Owners on a single phase or a backlog idea: specs only for now (review 2026-10-17)
