The board page, after the Academy board

I went through the board on port 4777: By status, By scope, an opened card and the client export. This is the design redrawn with what works there, at the size your real board has (26 projects, 99 cards).

Settled on 2026-11-13: the Now screen (triage + sessions), one click (answer a waiting question), agents read the same board.

New here: the Plan view, by project and priority, and what a card shows when you open it.

Changed after "too tight and too monotone": the Academy board's colours are back, each with one meaning, and every row has room.

What I took, what I left

Taken from By scope

  • One row per project, P0 first. A project is a tag.
  • The reason next to the priority. "Booking widget stops when Mindbody ends."
  • The count line and bar: to do, moving, done.
  • The colours: orange to do, blue moving, green done, and coloured deadline chips.
  • Panels inside a row: who has what, then Open with a dashed edge for asks nobody holds.
  • Deadline words: overdue, today or tomorrow, a date, no deadline yet.
  • Done as a count you can open.

Left behind

  • Every form: Edit, tags, Claimed by, Assignee, move, Delete, Add a card. Sessions write those.
  • The hand-written status paragraph per project. It is replaced by phase squares that are computed, so they can't go stale.
  • Every row open at once. By scope is 9,800 px tall today. Rows start folded.
  • Red on P0. Red means trouble only: overdue, or a session gone silent. P0 is the heaviest badge.
  • By status as three full columns. Now covers it.

How to read a row

Bar. Green is done, blue is moving, orange is to do.
Squares. One per phase: to do, doing, built, live.
ATDisc. Violet is Anton, pink is Taras. Solid means a session is writing now; dashed means nobody.
overduetodayRed. Trouble: overdue, due today, or a session gone silent.
waits on youYellow. Waits on you, and nothing else.
P0P1Badge. Priority by weight: P0 is solid, P1 an outline.

Where each part comes from

  • Project, priority and the reason: the tag. A session writes the reason when you give it.
  • A panel: the spec that covers those cards, with its live session.
  • Open: cards no spec covers, or no session holds.
  • Counts, squares and "who": computed. Nobody types them.

Choices I made; say if any is wrong

  • A card with two tags shows in both projects, as on the Academy board.
  • Rows start folded. A row opens by itself when something in it is overdue or waits on you.
  • Sort by priority, with a switch to sort by deadline. Inside a project: moving first, then by date.
  • Done cards are a count until you click.
  • P0 is the heaviest badge, not a red one, so red only ever means trouble. Say so if you want the red P0 stripe back.
  • Today and tomorrow are a red outline, not orange, because orange means to do.
  • No client export in this phase. The Academy board has one; I'll log it as a card with the session-tab jump.
  • The Academy board keeps running as it is until phase 11 moves its cards over.

Say "build it", or tell me what to change.
