## Success metrics

- In CRM, the week after the switch: no session offers a finished phase as ready, and no hand-off prints
  `diverged on main in …/progress.md`.
- Spec-docs-only commits on CRM's main fall from about 60 a day (419 of 508 in the week to 2026-11-11) to under 20.
- "What's left" and "where is PR C" are answered from one command, with no lookups across worktrees.
- Every task marked done has a commit on the remote; no CRM worktree holds unpushed commits older than a day.
- Academy's 90 cards are on the one board under an Academy Ballroom tag, and the old board repo is archived.
